# Social brand artwork

[OUR CHOICE] Facebook, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, YouTube, WhatsApp, Pinterest and Telegram
SVGs are copied unchanged from [Simple Icons 13.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.21.0/icons).
The upstream CC0 dedication is included in LICENSE.md. Brand trademarks remain with their
owners. Assets are served locally and used as monochrome masks in the footer and CMS.

Instagram and TikTok continue to use the existing assets in public/email/social.

Email footer variants are generated by `scripts/build-social-icons.mjs` as local PNGs
in `public/email/social`. They use the same social artwork, existing Instagram/TikTok
glyphs and the installed Lucide icons for website, email, phone, location and custom
links. The three styles are WellPlace colour, platform colours and monochrome.
